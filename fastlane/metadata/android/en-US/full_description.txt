CodeKeyboard is a programmer-focused Android keyboard inspired by the Sofle split mechanical keyboard layout.

Features:
- Sofle-style split layout with home-row modifiers (Ctrl, Alt, Meta, Shift on hold)
- Multiple layers: Base, Lower (symbols/numbers), Raise (navigation/F-keys), Adjust (media), Func (editor shortcuts)
- Emoji panel with category tabs and skin tone variants
- Word suggestions with fuzzy matching
- Dark theme designed for long coding sessions
- No internet permission, no telemetry, no ads

This is a fully offline, privacy-respecting keyboard. No data ever leaves your device.
